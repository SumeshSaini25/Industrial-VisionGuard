
# Metal Nut Visual Quality Inspection Guide

## 1. Purpose

This document provides general guidance for visual inspection
of industrial metal nuts.

It is an educational reference for the Industrial VisionGuard
project, not an approved manufacturing inspection standard.

## 2. Surface Scratches

Description:
A scratch is a line-shaped surface mark or abrasion.

Possible causes:
- Contact with sharp tools
- Friction during material handling
- Contact with machinery
- Improper storage or transportation

Recommended inspection:
- Examine the affected surface under suitable lighting.
- Check whether the scratch affects a functional surface.
- Compare the defect with approved reference samples.
- Escalate uncertain cases for manual inspection.

## 3. Bent or Deformed Components

Description:
A bent component has an unexpected change in shape
or geometry.

Possible causes:
- Excessive mechanical force
- Improper handling
- Tooling misalignment
- Manufacturing process variation

Recommended inspection:
- Check the component's geometry.
- Verify critical dimensions using suitable instruments.
- Compare against the approved engineering drawing.
- Escalate suspected deformation for review.

## 4. Surface Color Variations

Description:
Unexpected color differences may indicate surface
finish variation, discoloration, or contamination.

Possible causes:
- Surface contamination
- Coating inconsistencies
- Oxidation
- Process variation

Recommended inspection:
- Inspect under consistent lighting.
- Check the surface finish.
- Compare against approved reference samples.
- Investigate potential contamination.

## 5. Incorrect Orientation

Description:
A component may be positioned differently from
the expected inspection orientation.

Possible causes:
- Incorrect placement
- Feeding or alignment problems
- Handling errors

Recommended inspection:
- Verify the expected orientation.
- Check the feeding or positioning system.
- Reposition the component and inspect again.

## 6. Quality-Control Decision Guidelines

PASS:
No unacceptable visual anomaly is detected according
to the applicable approved inspection criteria.

REVIEW:
A suspected anomaly requires further examination.

FAIL:
A confirmed defect violates approved acceptance criteria.

Important:
Industrial VisionGuard's PatchCore model detects visual
anomalies. It does not independently establish the
physical cause, severity, or engineering acceptability
of a component.

Final acceptance criteria must come from the relevant
approved engineering specifications.

## 7. General Corrective Actions

- Review the manufacturing process.
- Inspect handling and storage conditions.
- Check tooling and equipment.
- Compare findings against approved quality standards.
- Document recurring anomalies.
- Request qualified engineering review when necessary.
